# Platform audit (Nov 5, 2026)

Full pass over the lead flow: campaign reply → assistant → form → chase → applied → Ken, plus forms, calls,
automations, sending paths, compliance, worker, reports and the CRM UI.

## Bugs and wiring problems → fixes

| # | Problem | Risk | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A due follow-up could still go out in the 40 s while a driver's new text waits for the assistant (debounce). Driver texts "yes", then gets "still looking?" a few seconds later. | Looks robotic | When a driver writes, pending follow-ups are cancelled right away (the reply re-plans them). |
| 2 | New contacts from texts, calls, forms and inbound email have no state, so no time zone, so quiet hours never applied to the texts we start. Follow-ups could go out at 3am. | TCPA / annoyance | Unknown time zone: our texts only go out when it's 8am–9pm everywhere in the US (11am–9pm Eastern), and follow-ups are timed on Central time. |
| 3 | The new Resend email fallback could send cold email from our main domain to leads who never wrote to us (for example an automation "send email" step on an imported lead). | Domain reputation | Direct email only to drivers who engaged (wrote, called, filled a form, came from the website). Cold leads stay on Instantly. |
| 4 | Hand-off words were too broad: "are you human?" and "is this a scam?" sent the driver to a person instead of an honest answer. | Lost drivers | Hand off only for asking to talk to a person/manager, legal words, threats. Skeptic questions are answered. |
| 5 | `DEEPSEEK_MONTHLY_CAP_USD` was documented but not enforced. | Surprise bill | Enforced: over the cap the assistant pauses replies, drivers get a person, Ken gets one Telegram alert. |
| 6 | Dashboard funnel skipped Following up, Qualified and With Ken, so counts after "Form sent" were too low. | Wrong numbers | Funnel counts every stage at or past each step. |
| 7 | An SMS to a contact with no phone number crashed the send instead of failing cleanly. | Stuck outbox row | Clear "no phone number" failure. |
| 8 | A driver who texts while a person has taken over (2 h pause) got no reply and nobody was told. | Driver left hanging | Task "Driver replied while you were handling" + appears in the inbox as before. |
| 9 | Automation "Move stage" didn't fire other automations that start on a stage change. | Broken chains | Emits the stage-change event like every other move. |
| 10 | Automation "Alert Ken" sent email + text but not Telegram. | Missed alerts | Also posts to Ken's Telegram group. |
| 11 | Built-in automation texted "Ken has been trying to reach you" even when he hadn't called yet. | Untrue message | Reworded: "Ken wants to go over your application. What time works for a quick call?" |
| 12 | Our own assistant emails sent through Instantly could show twice on the timeline when the sync pulls them back. | Messy timeline | Outbound email already recorded in the last 30 min with the same text is skipped. |
| 14 | Live test: asked "am I talking to a bot?", the assistant answered "real person here". | Dishonest, and illegal in some states (e.g. California bot disclosure) | It now says plainly it's an AI assistant that helps Ken; claiming to be human is blocked in code. |
| 13 | Leftovers: unused `ASSISTANT_DRAFT_MODE` env key (draft mode lives in the CRM), stale "phase 4" comment. | Confusion | Removed. |

## Checked and fine
- Consent: texts only with consent (inbound text/call, form box, "text me" / cell in an email); STOP and plain-language opt-outs; suppressions; replies 24/7, our own texts 8am–9pm driver time.
- Stage rules: automatic moves only forward; opt-out wins; nurture → replied when they come back.
- Form completion stops every follow-up and pending assistant message, sends the confirmation, alerts Ken.
- Kill switch: env + CRM switch both required; outbox waits while off; stale assistant messages dropped on re-enable.
- Forms: server-side validation, honeypot, rate limit, cached public pages expire on save, Spanish stored as English values.
- Production build passes; Supabase advisors clean except leaked-password protection (dashboard switch).
